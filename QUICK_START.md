# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - BioRoots

## What You Can Do Now

### 1. ⏰ Log Your Times (GMT+3)
- **Wake-up time**: Enter hour & minute when you woke up
- **Sleep time**: Enter hour & minute when you went to bed
- Get scored on consistency (25 points each)

### 2. 🍽️ Log Meals with Full Analysis

**What to Enter:**
```
Meal Type: [Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snack]
Ingredients: chicken breast, brown rice, broccoli, olive oil
Calories: 500 kcal
Quantity: 300 g
Composition: [Protein-heavy/Balanced/Carb-heavy]
```

**What You Get:**
- ✅ **Biology Level**: OPTIMAL/GOOD/MODERATE/POOR
- 📊 **Impact Score**: 0-100 points
- 🔴 **Problems Detected**:
  - Glucose spike risks
  - Afternoon slump warnings
  - Brain fog alerts
  - Neurotransmitter concerns
- ✅ **Benefits Identified**:
  - Good protein sources
  - Healthy fats
  - Optimal timing
  - Right calorie range
- 💡 **Recommendations**:
  - What to add/remove
  - Better timing suggestions
  - Ingredient swaps

### 3. 📊 Track Your Daily Score

Your score is based on:
- ⏰ Wake time consistency (25 pts)
- 😴 Sleep time consistency (25 pts)
- 🍽️ Number of meals (30 pts)
- 📏 Meal quantities (10 pts)
- 🚶 Movement frequency (10 pts)

**Total: 100 points → Letter Grade (A+ to D)**

## Common Meal Examples

### ✅ OPTIMAL Breakfast (95/100)
```
Ingredients: eggs, avocado, whole grain toast, spinach
Calories: 450 kcal
Quantity: 250g
Composition: Protein-heavy

Why it's optimal:
✓ Protein sets dopamine tone for the day
✓ Healthy fats slow glucose absorption
✓ Perfect calorie range
✓ Sustained energy without crash
```

### 🔴 POOR Lunch (35/100)
```
Ingredients: white rice, pasta, soda
Calories: 650 kcal
Quantity: 400g
Composition: Carb-heavy
Time: 13:00 (during work hours)

Why it's problematic:
✗ Glucose spike → Insulin surge → Sleepiness
✗ Afternoon slump risk (lunch + circadian dip)
✗ No protein → Neurotransmitter production impaired
✗ Large portion → Blood diverted from brain → Brain fog

Fix: Add protein, reduce portion, save carbs for evening
```

### ✅ GOOD Dinner (85/100)
```
Ingredients: salmon, sweet potato, vegetables, olive oil
Calories: 550 kcal
Quantity: 350g
Composition: Balanced
Time: 20:00

Why it's good:
✓ Protein + healthy fats
✓ Evening carbs help sleep preparation
✓ Good calorie range
✓ Optimal timing for this composition
```

## Key Rules for High Scores

### 🏆 For OPTIMAL Biology Scores:

1. **Breakfast**: Protein-heavy (eggs, Greek yogurt, chicken)
2. **Lunch**: Protein-heavy or balanced (avoid carb-heavy!)
3. **Dinner**: Balanced or carb-heavy is OK
4. **Snacks**: <300 kcal, protein if possible

### ⚠️ Avoid These During Work Hours (9am-5pm):

- ❌ Carb-heavy meals (especially lunch)
- ❌ Refined carbs without protein (white rice, pasta, bread alone)
- ❌ Large portions >500g
- ❌ High-calorie meals >800 kcal

### ✅ Do This During Work Hours:

- ✅ Protein with every meal
- ✅ Moderate portions (200-400g)
- ✅ Pair carbs with protein/fat
- ✅ 400-600 kcal per meal

## Biological Warnings Explained

### 🔴 "GLUCOSE SPIKE RISK"
**What it means**: Refined carbs without protein buffer

**What happens in your body**:
```
Refined carbs
    ↓
Rapid insulin surge
    ↓
Tryptophan crosses blood-brain barrier
    ↓
Serotonin → Melatonin
    ↓
SLEEPINESS
```

**Fix**: Add protein or healthy fats to slow absorption

### 🔴 "AFTERNOON SLUMP RISK"
**What it means**: Carb-heavy lunch at 12-2pm

**What happens in your body**:
```
Carb-heavy lunch at 1pm
    +
Natural afternoon circadian dip (2-3pm)
    ↓
SEVERE DROWSINESS
    ↓
Can't focus or work effectively
```

**Fix**: Protein-rich lunch during work hours, save carbs for dinner

### ⚠️ "BRAIN FOG"
**What it means**: Meal portion too large

**What happens in your body**:
```
Large meal (>500g)
    ↓
Digestion needs more blood flow
    ↓
Blood diverted from brain to stomach
    ↓
Less oxygen to prefrontal cortex
    ↓
MENTAL SLUGGISHNESS
```

**Fix**: Moderate portions, especially during work
